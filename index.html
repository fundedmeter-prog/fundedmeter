01 · HEADLINE FEATURE

The “Room left today” meter

One big number: how much you can still lose today before you break your firm's rules. It takes whichever is tighter, your daily loss limit or your trailing drawdown floor.

  • Green = full room. Yellow = size down. Red = done for the day.
  • Shows how many full stops you have left at your risk per trade
  • Updates the moment you log a trade
02

Prop rules panel, with your firm's numbers

Type in the rules from your evaluation or funded account. No firm's rules are hard-coded, because firms change them.

  • Daily loss limit with reset time
  • Max drawdown: static, trailing intraday or trailing end-of-day
  • Profit target progress and minimum trading days
03

Futures position sizer

Type your stop in points. Get the max contracts that fit your risk per trade, rounded down. Point values for NQ, MNQ, ES and MES are preloaded.

  • Flags when one contract already exceeds your risk rule
  • Warns when a trade risks more than today's room left
  • Shows the math, so you can check it
04

Discipline tracking: the cost of rule breaks

Tag each trade “rules followed: yes/no” and pick a reason. FundedMeter totals what breaking your own rules has cost you, by reason.

  • Followed vs. broken P&L, side by side
  • Itemized: revenge trades, moved stops, oversizing…
  • A rules-followed % you can work on

FAQ

Questions

Is it an app?

No. It's a Google Sheets template (Excel-compatible). You make a copy, enter your rules once, then log trades. Nothing to install, no account to create, no subscription.

Does it work with my prop firm?

It works with any prop firm's rules that you enter yourself: daily loss limit, static or trailing drawdown, profit target and minimum days. We don't hard-code any firm's rules (they change), and we're not affiliated with any prop firm.

Does it connect to my broker or platform?

Not in v1. You log trades in the sheet with drop-downs (instrument, direction, contracts, entry, stop, exit), and everything else calculates automatically.

Only NQ / MNQ?

It's built NQ-first, with NQ, MNQ, ES and MES point values preloaded. You can add other instruments in Settings.

Is this financial advice?

No. FundedMeter is an educational, record-keeping and calculation tool. It doesn't give signals, trade ideas or recommendations, and it can't guarantee you'll pass an evaluation or keep an account. Always check your firm's official rules.

Are the numbers in the screenshots real?

No. All dashboard numbers shown are fictional demo data, and the images are design mockups of the planned template.

When does it launch, and what does joining cost?

Joining is free. Waitlist members get one email when it launches, with the $12 early-bird link. If there isn't enough interest, we'll let you know and won't email you again.
